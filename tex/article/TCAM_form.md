Section Policy

The main section is for submitting articles for evaluation by TCAM. Authors should select this section, except in specific cases of submissions from congresses supported by SBMAC.
Submission Requirements
You must read and acknowledge that you've completed the requirements below before proceeding.

    Works must be prepared following the guidelines in the template provided by the journal in TCAM.zip. Works created using other models or modifications of this model will not be accepted.
    The manuscript must be written in 10pt font size, maintaining a printing area of 19.0cm by 12.7cm and free margins according to the template.

    It is recommended that the manuscript be written in English. If the manuscript is written in Portuguese, it is necessary to include an abstract in English at the end of the text.
    Manuscripts cannot exceed a maximum of 20 pages. Cases that do not respect this restriction may be rejected at the editors' discretion.
    Figures must be prepared in color and in PNG or PDF format. Numbers, letters, and symbols in figures must be perfectly legible and in size compatible with that used in the text.
    Authors must ensure they have registered all authors in the metadata fields on the website, including full affiliation and ORCID identifiers.
    In the cover letter, the authors should list three possible reviewers in the same research area as the work being considered for publication.
